# Guard Companion free pilot — customer notice (draft)

**Status:** Draft for ISDL and qualified local review before use.
**Version:** 0.1 — 16 September 2026

Guard Companion, provided by Integrated Systems and Devices Limited (ISDL), helps you supervise guards you already employ. It records guard shift starts and ends, patrol/checkpoint activity, reported problems, supervisor resolution notes and supporting recordings or photos.

## What the pilot includes

The free pilot supports one property and up to five guards and supervisors combined. No payment is collected for the pilot. ISDL will tell you before a material change to these pilot limits or to the service.

## Information recorded

The service stores account and property information, shift and patrol records, report text, recordings, photos, and point-in-time location information taken when a guard starts/ends a shift or scans a checkpoint, where the device allows it. It also records when the device captured a record and when the service received it.

Owners and supervisors with access to the property can see its security records. Guards see only their active-shift work and their own current-shift reports. ISDL support staff access information only when needed to operate or support the pilot.

## Important limits

Guard Companion does not replace CCTV, physical access control, emergency response, a security provider, or site emergency procedures. A patrol record or checkpoint scan does not prove that a property is secure. Location may be unavailable or inaccurate. Mobile-browser uploads may wait until the device reconnects and the app is opened again.

For urgent safety concerns, guards must use the site's configured telephone contact and local emergency procedure. The pilot does not dispatch police, guarantee monitoring, or guarantee that a record was delivered or reviewed.

## Retention, help and leaving the pilot

Pilot records are retained for one year unless ISDL tells you in writing that a different requirement applies. Contact **[ISDL support contact to be inserted]** for support, a request for an export, or to leave the pilot. ISDL will verify the requesting owner before providing an export or ending access.

Before using Guard Companion, please provide the property address, assign responsible supervisors, configure the site escalation contact, and explain these point-in-time collections and the site procedure to participating guards.
